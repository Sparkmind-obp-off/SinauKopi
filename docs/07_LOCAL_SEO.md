# Local SEO

## Discovery intent
Primary terms include: Sinau Kopi Kembaran, SK Sinau Kopi, kedai kopi Kembaran, and coffee shop Kembaran Banyumas.

## On-page requirements
- Unique title and meta description.
- One clear H1.
- Location naturally included in relevant copy.
- Descriptive image alt text.
- Open Graph metadata.
- Canonical URL.
- Sitemap and robots configuration when applicable.
- Fast mobile performance.

## Structured data
Use appropriate Schema.org LocalBusiness/Cafe data only when it accurately reflects verified business information.

## Consistency
Keep name, address, phone, URL and opening hours consistent with official business profiles.

SEO must not introduce unsupported claims or keyword stuffing.
