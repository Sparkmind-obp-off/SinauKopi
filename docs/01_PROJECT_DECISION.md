# Project Decision — Sinau Kopi

**Status:** Approved foundation
**Scope:** Public-facing business website

## Decisions
1. Build a dedicated website for SK Sinau Kopi.
2. Mobile-first is mandatory.
3. Local discovery and conversion are primary: menu, location, hours, contact and directions.
4. Walk-in remains supported; do not imply online booking unless confirmed.
5. The website complements existing social/digital channels.
6. Verify business facts before publication.
7. Unknown data is marked TBD, never fabricated.
8. Keep the first release fast, accessible, SEO-ready and easy to maintain.

## Non-goals
- Full POS.
- Customer accounts.
- Online ordering without an approved workflow.
- Invented brand history, menu data or claims.
- Unnecessary technical complexity.

## Acceptance
A visitor can understand what Sinau Kopi is, discover relevant offerings, find when/where to visit, and contact the business within a few taps.
