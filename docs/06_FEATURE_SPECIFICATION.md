# Feature Specification

## MVP
- Business identity: name, logo and description.
- Menu: verified categories, items, descriptions, prices and optional photos.
- Location: address/area, map link and directions CTA.
- Operating hours: verified current hours only.
- Contact: WhatsApp, phone and social links when confirmed.
- Gallery: optimized real venue/product imagery.
- Announcements: optional verified promo/event area.
- SEO/social metadata.

## Later
Menu CMS, online ordering, reservations, membership, events and analytics.

## Exclusions
No payment collection or customer-account system in MVP unless separately approved.
