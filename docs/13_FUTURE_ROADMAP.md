# Future Roadmap

## Stage 1 — Digital presence
Website, accurate menu, location, hours, contact, gallery and social links.

## Stage 2 — Conversion
Promotions, announcements, WhatsApp inquiry flow and campaign landing pages.

## Stage 3 — Retention
Membership information, loyalty mechanics and event/program pages.

## Stage 4 — Transactions
Online ordering/reservations only if the business has a clear operational process and the feature solves a real customer need.

## Stage 5 — Measurement
Privacy-conscious analytics, conversion events, campaign attribution and content iteration.

## Decision rule
Do not add complexity because it is technically possible. Add a feature when a verified business or customer need justifies its maintenance cost.
