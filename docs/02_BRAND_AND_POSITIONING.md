# Brand & Positioning

## Working identity
**Brand:** SK Sinau Kopi  
**Category:** Coffee shop / kedai kopi  
**Area:** Kembaran, Banyumas, Central Java, Indonesia

## Positioning direction
A real local coffee-shop presence: approachable, credible, contemporary and useful.

## Voice
- Natural Indonesian.
- Warm, not exaggerated.
- Short and concrete.
- Local and human.
- No unsupported superlatives.

## Visual direction
Use the shop's real identity, logo and photography as the source of truth. Avoid a generic or artificially premium identity.

## Required source assets
- Official logo.
- Current storefront/interior photos.
- Menu photos.
- Official social links.
- Verified contact and location.
